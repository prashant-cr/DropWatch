# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume they are acting in good faith. Argue about code, not
about each other.

## What that means in practice

Welcome: direct technical disagreement, saying "I think this is the wrong approach
and here is why", asking questions that seem obvious, and changing your mind.

Not welcome: personal attacks, demeaning or exclusionary comments, harassment of any
kind, publishing anyone's private information, and sustained disruption of
discussions. Insisting a maintainer owes you their time also falls here — this is a
project people work on for free.

This applies in issues, pull requests, commit messages, and anywhere else the project
is being discussed.

## Scope

These expectations hold in all project spaces, and in public spaces when you are
representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report problems to <sutharprashant199722@gmail.com>. Reports stay confidential.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, code, and issues that do not follow
this document, and may ban anyone whose behaviour is abusive or persistently
disruptive. In practice the response scales with the problem: usually a note about
what was out of line, occasionally a temporary block, rarely a permanent one.
